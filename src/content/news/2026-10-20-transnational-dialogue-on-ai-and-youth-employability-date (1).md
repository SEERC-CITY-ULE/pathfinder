summary: On 20 October 2026, the PATHFINDER project held its first Transnational Dialogue on AI and Youth Employability in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, hosted by CREDI. The event brought together young people, employers, career advisors, researchers, project partners and Advisory Board members to exchange perspectives on the opportunities and challenges of an AI-driven labour market.

Building on national research findings and dialogues conducted in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Serbia and Greece, the event provided an opportunity to move from national experiences towards a shared understanding of the challenges facing young people across Europe. Each partner country was represented by young people, bringing together representatives who shared their experiences, perspectives and ideas throughout the dialogue. Their participation ensured that **young people's voices remained central** to discussions about the future of employment.

**From National Insights to Shared Priorities**

The event opened with presentations of findings from the five partner countries, highlighting common concerns around AI literacy, changing employment opportunities, digital inequalities and the growing need for education systems to adapt to technological developments.

Participants then engaged in three interactive roundtable discussions, exploring:

- **From Education to First Meaningful Work:** How AI is transforming entry-level employment and young people's transition into the labour market.
- **Beyond AI Skills:** The importance of critical thinking, adaptability, responsible AI use and lifelong learning in preparing for future careers.
- **Who Gets Left Behind?** The barriers faced by young people at risk of exclusion and possible measures to ensure equal access to emerging opportunities.

**From Dialogue to Action**

The discussions highlighted a key message: **preparing young people for an AI-driven future requires more than technological skills**. It calls for inclusive opportunities, practical experience and stronger collaboration between education, employers and policymakers.

The event concluded with a dedicated Advisory Board meeting, where project partners and board members reflected on the discussions and considered the next steps.

The insights gathered in Sarajevo will contribute to PATHFINDER's upcoming research, policy recommendations and activities, supporting a more inclusive and youth-centred approach to the future of employment.
