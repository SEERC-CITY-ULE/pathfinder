Today the **PATHFINDER** project officially begins. Funded by the European Union under the CERV programme (grant `101253819`), the project will run until **31 March 2028**.

Over the next 24 months, six partner organisations — European Dialogue (Slovakia, coordinator), CREDI (Bosnia & Herzegovina), CITY ULE with SEERC (Greece), Light Trees / Fotódentra (Greece), PCO (Bulgaria), and Young Researchers of Serbia — will work with hundreds of young people aged 18–30, especially those from marginalised backgrounds, to ask one urgent question:

> Is AI a crisis or an opportunity for the future of work?

## What's coming next

- **Months 1–4**: Participatory research events in each partner country, co-designed with youth and employers.
- **Month 7**: The transnational dialogue in Sarajevo.
- **Months 7–11**: Five national public debates on AI and the future of work.
- **Month 20**: The PATHFINDER Sofia Forum.
- **Month 24**: The EU Policy Roundtable.
