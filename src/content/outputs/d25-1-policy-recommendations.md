The closing output of the PATHFINDER project — and the one with the longest reach.

The **PATHFINDER Policy Recommendation Report** consolidates everything the project has learned, heard, and tested over 24 months into a concrete set of recommendations for EU and national policymakers on:

- Bridging the AI skills gap among young people, especially those most at risk of exclusion.
- Embedding youth voices in AI-related policy consultations as a default, not an exception.
- Aligning education and vocational training reforms with the realities of AI-driven labour markets.
- Designing inclusive employment pathways for marginalised youth.

The report is formally presented at the **EU Policy Roundtable** (January 2028) and shared with EU institutions, national ministries, and partner networks for adaptation across Europe.
