The PATHFINDER Sofia Forum is the flagship event where youth, employers, EU policymakers, and Advisory Board members co-create a shared response to AI-driven labour-market change.

The **Sofia Event Report** documents the plenary outcomes and design-thinking workshops. Its companion output — the **Youth Skills Lab Curriculum** — translates the Forum's conclusions into a hands-on training scheme to be delivered in five national Skills Labs (Bosnia & Herzegovina, Greece, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Serbia) over the following two months.

The curriculum is anchored in four pillars:

1. **Basic AI literacy** — what AI is, what it isn't, what it can and cannot do.
2. **4th-generation skills** — adaptability, critical thinking, creativity, emotional intelligence.
3. **Career readiness in AI-driven sectors** — navigating evolving role definitions, employer expectations, and the EU policy landscape.
4. **Ethical and civic competence** — understanding bias, transparency, and youth rights in AI systems.
