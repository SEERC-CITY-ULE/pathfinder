Following the country-level participatory research events held in Bosnia & Herzegovina, Greece, Bulgaria, Slovakia, and Serbia, the **Transnational Research Synthesis Report** brings the five strands together into a single, comparable picture.

It captures:

- Cross-country findings on how young people perceive AI's impact on their employment prospects.
- A shared research agenda for the rest of the project lifecycle.
- Best practices and inclusive methodologies for engaging marginalised youth — to be reused in subsequent national debates and Skills Labs.
- Recommendations on which skills are most urgent for young people to develop.

The report will be made public after the Sarajevo transnational dialogue.
