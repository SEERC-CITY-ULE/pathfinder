The closing Policy Roundtable is intentionally online so that participants from across the consortium, the Advisory Board, EU institutions, and youth networks can join without travel barriers. Inclusivity sits at the heart of the project — that includes accessibility of the events themselves.

The Roundtable presents the PATHFINDER Policy Recommendation Report — built from national debates, the Sofia Forum, and transnational dialogue — alongside lived-experience contributions from youth participants across the five project countries and reflections from the Advisory Board on AI development, regulation, employability counselling, and work-based learning.
