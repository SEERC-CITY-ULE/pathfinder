## Why online

The closing Policy Roundtable is intentionally online so that participants from across the consortium, the Advisory Board, EU institutions, and youth networks can join without travel barriers. Inclusivity sits at the heart of the project — that includes accessibility of the events themselves.

## What gets presented

- The PATHFINDER Policy Recommendation Report (D25.1), built from national debates, the Sofia Forum, and transnational dialogue.
- Lived experience contributions from youth participants in the five project countries.
- Reflections from the Advisory Board across AI development, regulation, employability counselling, and work-based learning.
