The event will bring together young people, including young people with disabilities, employers, education and career advisors, researchers, human resources professionals and policymakers to **exchange experiences** and perspectives on the **future of employment**.

Discussions will address the skills, opportunities and barriers associated with accessing the labour market, highlighting the importance of inclusion.

The event is organised by **Light Trees (Fotodentra)** in collaboration with **CITY ULE – University of York Europe Campus** and will be hosted at **Myrtillo**.
