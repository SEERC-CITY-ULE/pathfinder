## What to expect

Plenary sessions and design-thinking workshops alongside youth, employers, and policymakers. The Forum makes sure that the voices of those most affected by AI-driven changes in the labour market are actively involved in shaping policy responses.

Advisory Board members will contribute expert perspectives across cutting-edge AI development, regulation and ethics, youth employability counselling, and work-based learning.

## Outputs

The Sofia Forum feeds directly into the Youth Skills Lab curriculum (D19.1) and the PATHFINDER Policy Recommendation Report (D25.1).
