CREDI investigates how AI is reshaping employment, helping young people develop future-ready skills and creating participatory learning spaces that build resilience, adaptability, and informed career decision-making. Recent work on forecasting future labour market needs (SMARTLABOR) directly informs PATHFINDER's research agenda.
