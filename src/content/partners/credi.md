summary: CREDI is an independent research institute in Bosnia and Herzegovina conducting applied social science research. Its work covers areas including labour markets, migration, education, social protection and socio-economic development. CREDI coordinates the Western Balkan Migration Network and has developed research infrastructure supporting access to social science data in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Its research experience includes national, regional and international work on labour-market developments, migration, youth employability and digital transformation.
