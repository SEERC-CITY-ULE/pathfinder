The South-East European Research Centre (SEERC), CITY ULE's award-winning research arm, has implemented nearly 100 European projects (Horizon, CERV, JUST, Erasmus+) — including the LISTEN project on civic engagement and ACCTING on the impact of the Green Deal on vulnerable populations. SEERC's annual Career Days and partnerships with Chambers of Commerce give PATHFINDER direct lines to youth entering the labour market.
